This paper studies the perception bottleneck of VLMs for chart understanding. Junteng Liu is the first author; co-authors are Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, and Junxian He. It was published on Arxiv in 2025. The GitHub code repository is Vision4Chart: [Vision4Chart](https://github.com/Vicent0205/Vision4Chart).
