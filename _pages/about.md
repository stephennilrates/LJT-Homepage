I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://www.cs.ust.hk/~junxianh/), supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://www.cs.ust.hk/~junxianh/). My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with particular interests in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
